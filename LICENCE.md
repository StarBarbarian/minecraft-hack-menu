## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Coordinate Switcher — jump to any saved position in single-player
- Block Hitboxes — visualize block boundaries
- Replay Notes — record and review movement paths
- Playback Speed — adjust game speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Item Catalog — browse items, colors, enchantments, effects
- Custom Skin Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Night Vision — improved visibility in dark areas

### Aimbot
- Creative Mode Notes — segment study on any build
- Gravity Control — manually switch gravity mid-game
- Jump Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the world
